= Searching for Services

include::_attributes.adoc[]
endif::[]

From the *Home* and *Services* pages, search the catalog by a service name to find a specific service. Enter at least four characters in the *Search All Services* box then select an option from the results.

image::aeh-search-by-api-name-box.png["Search box to enter a service name",20%]

Filter the service by category and type to further refine your search results. The filter options display depending on which service categories and types are available in the portal.

To filter by categories or tags, select one or more options from *All categories* or *All service types* and click *Apply*.

From the *Home* page, you can also filter the carousel by clicking on any of the service type cards.

image::aeh-api-type-filter.png["Service cards you can select to filter results by service type"]
